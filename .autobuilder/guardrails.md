# Cheetah Bear Fuel Guardrails

This is a beverage and lifestyle brand, not SaaS.

The public site should sell brand identity, product desire, and waitlist conversion. It should not talk about internal automation, recovery work, founder operating systems, or Autobuilder.

## Preserve
- Cheetah Bear Fuel brand identity.
- `CHEETAH BEAR FUEL`
- `TWO BEAST ONE CAN`
- `Why be one beast when you can be two?`
- `public/hero-cheetah-bear.png`
- Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, pnpm, and Vercel readiness.

## Do Not
- Do not expose Autobuilder.
- Do not delete the hero image.
- Do not use npm.
- Do not deploy automatically.
- Do not push automatically.
- Do not add fake checkout or fake inventory.
- Do not turn the site into generic SaaS.
- Do not soften the brand into corporate wellness.

## Operating Rules
- Use pnpm.
- Manual deployment only.
- Build after meaningful source changes.
- Keep Supabase optional at build time and only required when the waitlist route is called.
- Keep the public website focused on the beverage brand, product lines, lifestyle signal, and waitlist CTA.
